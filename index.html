FAQ

Answers before you install.

The small print for a paid desktop product — privacy, devices, refunds, updates.

Is the $59 a one-time payment?

Yes. Pay $59 once and Dictivo is yours. All v1.x point releases are included. v2.0 will be a paid upgrade at 50% off the then-current price for existing license holders.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

Dictivo keeps launching, but dictation is paused until a license is entered. Your settings, history, and local dictionary stay intact — enter a key and you pick up exactly where you left off.

How many devices can I use one license on?

Up to three personal devices — for example, a work Mac, a home Mac, and a Windows laptop. Need to move a license between machines? Deactivate the old device from settings and the slot frees up immediately.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked. Email support@dictivo.app and we'll process it.

Does my voice ever leave my computer?

No. Transcription runs locally on your device using the on-device engine. Dictivo only makes network requests for update checks and license activation. See the security page for the full breakdown.

Do you sell team or education licenses?

Yes. Volume pricing starts at five seats; education and non-profit pricing is available on request. Email support@dictivo.app with the number of seats and we'll send a quote.
