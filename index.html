FAQ

Answers before you install.

The small print for a paid desktop product — privacy, devices, refunds, updates, speed.

01 Does my voice ever leave my computer? +

No. Transcription runs locally on your device using the on-device engine. The only network traffic Dictivo makes is a license activation check and a periodic update manifest fetch — no audio, no transcripts, no metadata about what you said. See the security page for the full breakdown.

02 Why isn't it faster than a cloud transcription service? +

Because the speech model runs on your device, not on a GPU farm. That trade is the whole point of Dictivo — you give up a small amount of latency in exchange for never sending your voice off the machine. Standard mode is fast enough for drafting in real time; Accurate mode takes a little longer and is meant for clean exports.

03 Is the $59 a one-time payment? +

Yes. Pay $59 once and Dictivo is yours. All v1.x point releases are included. v2.0 will be a paid upgrade at 50% off the then-current price for existing license holders.

04 What happens when the 14-day trial ends? +

Dictivo keeps launching, but it pauses dictation until you enter a license. Your settings, history, and local dictionary stay intact — drop in a key and you pick up exactly where you left off.

05 How many devices can I use one license on? +

Up to 3 personal devices — for example, a work Mac, a home Mac, and a Windows laptop. Need to move a license between machines? Deactivate the old device from settings and the slot frees up immediately.

06 Can I get a refund? +

Yes — within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked. Email support@dictivo.app and we'll process it.

07 Do you sell team or education licenses? +

Yes. Volume pricing starts at 5 seats; education and non-profit pricing is available on request. Email support@dictivo.app with the number of seats and we'll send a quote.
