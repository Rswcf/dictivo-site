The displayed transcripts were produced by Local and Cloud Fast processing of
these recordings. The film presents those results in a designed interface with
shortened waits; it is not a continuous screen recording or a speed/comparative
accuracy benchmark. Scene titles are illustrative. Local keeps dictation audio
on-device after setup; Cloud Fast uploads the selected audio for transcription.
