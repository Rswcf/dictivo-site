Dictivo — Keep your flow. (68 seconds, 2026-11-12)

What is real
- Screen recordings of Dictivo 0.3.57 on macOS, made on 2026-11-12: Mail (a new
  message), Notes (three notes) and the Dictivo main window, with the Dictivo
  Companion.
- Every dictated text on screen is the text Dictivo pasted. The Mail scene and
  the first two Notes scenes used Local mode. The last Notes scene used
  Cloud Fast. The word counts in the Companion are the app's own.
- The main window scenes show real clicks from Private Local to Cloud Fast and
  back to Private Local.
- During recording, the generated voice files played through the Mac's
  speakers, and Dictivo transcribed them from the built-in microphone.
  Software sent the shortcut presses and the clicks.

What is generated
- Dictated voice: ElevenLabs text-to-speech, model eleven_v4, stock voice
  "Jessica". The film contains no recording of a human voice.
- Music: ElevenLabs Music, model music_v2_5.
- Sound effects (typing, key presses, clicks, tones): ElevenLabs sound
  generation, default model. They are editorial sounds, not the app's own
  sounds.

How the recordings were edited
- No speed change. Every recorded part plays at normal speed.
- Silent pauses after the speech were shortened. In Mail, 3.77 s of silent
  listening after the last word was cut and 0.80 s remains. The Notes and
  Prompts scenes show only the last 0.5 s of each dictation. The Cloud Fast
  scene shows the whole sentence.
- Waits are shown for at most 0.5 s. Time from the stop to the pasted text,
  real and shown:
    Mail, Local          0.23 s, shown in full
    Notes, Local         2.73 s, shown as 0.30 s
    Prompts, Local       3.27 s, shown as 0.30 s
    Notes, Cloud Fast    1.67 s, shown as 0.30 s
- The sender address in Mail is hidden under a box in the row's own colour.
  The "From:" label stays.
- In the Mail recording, the Companion sat to the right of the Mail window.
  Its frames from the same moment are placed over the empty lower right of
  the Mail window. Nothing in them is retimed or redrawn.
- The Notes and Prompts scenes play without the dictated voice.
- One Notes take was rejected: the app repeated the last clause (21 words
  instead of 15). It is not in the film. The file is kept on record.
- Titles, the keycap drawing and the check list are motion graphics. The
  cards at 32-34 s and the Companion avatar at 22-26 s and in the end card
  are still frames from the recordings.

Shortcut
- The titles name no key.
- The keycap drawing shows Dictivo's default dictation shortcut on Mac,
  Cmd+Shift+Space. On Windows the default is Ctrl+Shift+Space.
- The recordings show ⌘P, because ⌘P was the dictation shortcut set on the
  recording Mac.
- You can change the shortcut in Dictivo.

Local keeps dictation audio on the device after setup. Cloud Fast uploads the
recording you choose for transcription.
